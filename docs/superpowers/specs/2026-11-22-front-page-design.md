# Front page, logo and favicon

## Intent

Logged-out visitors to kortn.com land straight on Clerk's sign-in box, which still shows the old logo. The favicon and link preview are old too. kortn needs a proper front door that matches the deli branding (the Wes Anderson / Lox in a Box look, set in a Jewish home or card club). It's for two kinds of people: friends and family who've been invited to a table, and word-of-mouth visitors who may not know Kalooki.

**Success:** someone who has heard of kortn arrives and immediately knows where they are and what it is. Within one click they're signing up or signing in. Every place the brand shows up looks like the same place: tab, home screen, link previews, sign-in box and front page.

## Goals

- A front page at `/` for logged-out visitors (layout **B, "big sign, straight in"**, chosen from three mockups).
- A new favicon and home-screen icon (version **3, "cream sign, green K"**).
- The new logo (the green KORTN sign) in the sign-in and sign-up boxes, replacing Clerk's old logo.
- A new link-preview image for shared kortn.com links.

## Non-goals

- No change for signed-in players: `/` still shows the kitchen.
- No invite links, rules pages or marketing copy beyond a short "how it plays".
- No change to Clerk's sign-in flow itself (fields, providers).

## Anti-goals

- A page that feels like a SaaS landing page (feature grids, testimonials, pricing).
- Slowing down invited friends on their way to sign in.
- Logged-in players seeing the front page, even for a flash.

## Approaches considered

**Icons and link preview:**
- **A (chosen):** generated by the app with Next's metadata files (`app/icon.tsx`, `app/apple-icon.tsx`, `app/opengraph-image.tsx`) using `ImageResponse`, with the real Archivo Black font. One source of truth, easy to change.
- **B:** hand-drawn static PNG/SVG files. Slightly crisper at 16px, but every brand change means redrawing them.

**Front page location:** at `/` itself, with the server deciding between the front page and the kitchen by sign-in state. A separate `/welcome` route was rejected because it only adds a redirect.

**Sign-in box logo:** hide Clerk's own logo with `appearance` and hang our green KORTN sign (real font, HTML) above the box. Uploading a logo in the Clerk dashboard alone was rejected: it's a raster image, out of our code, and doesn't match exactly.

**Front page layout:** three mockups were compared:
- **A:** sign and menu card, like the kitchen.
- **B:** big sign, straight in.
- **C:** welcome beside the sign-in box.

The user chose **B**.

## Design

### Front page (`/`, logged out)

From the chosen mockup:

- **Background:** the dining-room photo, full screen, with the kitchen's ambient effects (`RoomBackdrop plate="room-home" ambient`, already pinned to the screen). No green header bar.
- **The sign:** a big green KORTN sign hanging from two gold chains at the top of the screen. It holds the wordmark (KORTN with THE / CARD / TABLE stacked) in cream, inside a cream inner outline. This sign is the new **logo**.
- **Tagline:** one line in the script font (Yesteryear), cream with a soft shadow: *"Kalooki with the family, wherever they are."* (wording is my call)
- **Two buttons:**
  - **Pull up a chair:** green pill with a cream ring, goes to `/sign-up`.
  - **Sign in:** cream outline pill, goes to `/sign-in`.
- **A cue at the foot of the first screen:** "▼ How it plays", which scrolls to the section below.
- **How it plays:** a cream card with a ribbon header (the kitchen's `MenuBoard` / `Ribbon` look), and three numbered steps in red circles. Copy is my call; rules checked against the game code (two packs, one joker each, 40 to open):
  1. **Set a table.** Share the code with family or friends.
  2. **Thirteen cards each,** two packs and the jokers. Lay down sets and runs; you need 40 to open.
  3. **Go out first,** keep your points low, and take the pot.

  Then one script line: *"Video on, kettle on. Deal again soon."* and the two buttons again.
- **Footer:** small print, "KORTN · Kalooki online".
- **Phones:** the sign scales down to fit the width, the tagline wraps, and the buttons stack full-width. The first screen still shows the sign, the tagline and both buttons without scrolling.
- **Motion:** the sign and card use the kitchen's existing `settle` entrance, and respect reduced motion as the kitchen does.

### Routing

- `/` becomes a public route in `proxy.ts` (exact match only). The page checks `auth()`:
  - **Signed in:** the kitchen, exactly as today.
  - **Signed out:** the front page.
- The redirect to `/sign-in` on `/` goes. Every other route keeps today's behaviour (logged-out visitors are sent to sign-in).

### Logo in the sign-in and sign-up boxes

- The sign-in and sign-up pages hang the same green KORTN sign above Clerk's box. Clerk's own logo is hidden through `appearance` (logo box element).
- A small "← kortn.com" link back to the front page is my call. It replaces the stray-visitor dead end.
- I'll also serve the logo as an image (`/logo.png`, generated the same way as the icons). You can upload it in the Clerk dashboard for the emails Clerk sends, which only the dashboard controls. That upload is a manual step for you.

### Favicon and home-screen icon (version 3)

- **Design:** a green (`#1d5446`) Archivo Black "K" on cream (`#f6efdc`), with a green rounded border, like the cream name plates.
- **Files:** `app/icon.tsx` (32×32 tab icon) and `app/apple-icon.tsx` (180×180), both via `ImageResponse` with the Archivo Black font.
- **Thin border at tab size:** at 32px the border is thickened so it still reads (the 32px column of the mockup shows the target).
- **Clean-up:**
  - Remove the old `public/art/favicon.svg` and `public/art/apple-touch-icon.png`.
  - Remove the `icons` entry in `app/layout.tsx` metadata, so the generated files are used.

### Link preview

- **Image:** `app/opengraph-image.tsx` at 1200×630. The dining-room photo, slightly darkened, with the green KORTN sign centred.
- **Text:**
  - Title: KORTN.
  - Description: *"Kalooki with the family, wherever they are."*
- **Clean-up:**
  - Remove the old `openGraph.images` (`/art/vercel-avatar.png`).
  - Delete that file if nothing else uses it.

### Fonts for generated images

`ImageResponse` needs the font file itself. Commit Archivo Black (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License) as a TTF in the repo and read it at build. Don't fetch it from Google at runtime.

## Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router. Read the metadata, `ImageResponse` and app-icon docs in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` first (AGENTS.md).
- Tailwind 4, plus the existing deli components and classes: `Wordmark`, `MenuBoard`, `Ribbon`, `.deli-pill`, fonts in `lib/ui/fonts.ts`.
- No production database writes. The front page reads no data.
- Brand colours: green `#1d5446`, cream `#f6efdc`, red `#c8402f`, gold `#e2bd4f`.

## Left to the builder

- Exact sizes and spacing, matched to the mockup (`.superpowers/brainstorm/…/front-page-layouts.html`, option B, and `logo-favicon.html`).
- Whether the hanging sign is a new shared component (front page, sign-in, OG image) or two variants of one.
- How to test:
  - Front page renders: sign, tagline, buttons pointing to `/sign-up` and `/sign-in`, the how-it-plays steps.
  - `/` chooses the front page or the kitchen by sign-in state.
  - The proxy treats only `/` as newly public.
  - Icons respond with images.
